One method, shared across the tools.

  1. 01 · MEASURE

    On our public test set

    Each tool measures every tier on the tool’s own public test set: 1,000 held-out records for the reader, 60 + 60 pairs, 42 + 42 cases, 84 files. Each rate carries its confidence interval.

    1,000 records · 120 for the generative tiers
  2. 02 · SEAL

    Hashed, then frozen

    Each record carries its content hash. A page checks it before a figure is shown. A record that changed after sealing is not used.

    content hash dbf26abe… (reader)
  3. 03 · RERUN

    On your own files, on your machine

    One command reruns the whole sweep on your records. The report is written next to your file, and no data leaves the network.

    run it yourself
    git clone https://github.com/ArslaneSempai-ui/crusetra-routingcd crusetra-routingnpm ci --ignore-scriptsnpm run measure:yours -- --cases=your-file.csv the report is written next to your file, and nowhere else
    4 commands, no account, no upload
  4. 04 · DOSSIER

    Signed, current, verifiable

    Four reports against five controls: present, frozen, signed with a key, fresh, and consistent. A reviewer checks the dossier from the hashes and signatures alone.

    4 of 4 reports · 5 controls
reports present: 4 of 4consistent control: 4 of 4

A reviewer does not want four files. The Dossier reads all four reports and answers on them as one. In this Dossier, all four reports are present, and all four hold the top control, consistent.

The tools run on your machine. Nothing reaches us unless you send it.
